Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Decoded Lemon ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Family Equity Kids (the "Kids App"), the children's companion app to Family Equity (the "Main App"). The Kids App is designed for children and is built around a simple principle: collect as little as possible. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no way for a child to enter personal information beyond short chore-request messages that are only visible to their own family.

Because the Kids App is used by children, it can only function after a parent or legal guardian deliberately sets it up. A parent must pair each device from their own Family Equity account, and this pairing constitutes the parent's consent to the limited data processing described below.

1. No Accounts, No Registration

The Kids App has no sign-up, login, email, password, or profile creation of any kind. A child cannot create an account or share personal details with us through the Kids App. All account-level information (the household, the parent's account, and each child's display name and avatar) is created and managed by a parent inside the Main App and is governed by the Main App's Terms and privacy practices.

2. Information the Kids App Processes

To show a child their chores, the Kids App accesses a narrow slice of the household data that a parent has already created in the Main App:

  • Pairing code: a short, single-use code generated by a parent in the Main App. It expires after one hour and is exchanged for a device token.
  • Device token: a random credential stored only on the paired device (in the operating system's secure storage). It identifies the device to our servers and is scoped to a single child in a single household. It is not an advertising identifier and is not shared with anyone.
  • Child display info: the first name or nickname and avatar that the parent chose for the child in the Main App.
  • Chore data: the chores assigned to that child, their schedules, and completion marks the child makes in the Kids App.
  • Chore requests: short messages a child sends to ask a parent to add a chore. These are visible only to the child's own household.

The Kids App does not collect or access: precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, browsing history, advertising identifiers, or any behavioural analytics. It requests no sensitive device permissions.

3. How Information Is Used

The information above is used solely to operate the app: to display the child's chores, record completions, deliver chore requests to the parent, and verify (on every request) that the device is still paired and the household's subscription is active. We do not use any of this data for advertising, profiling, or marketing, and we never sell or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

4. Children's Privacy and Parental Control

The Kids App is intended for children under parental supervision, and parents remain in control at all times:

  • Only a parent or legal guardian with a Family Equity account can pair a device — a child cannot connect the app on their own.
  • Parents can see every paired device in the Main App and can revoke access instantly at any time, which immediately cuts the device off from all household data.
  • All of the child's data is created, viewed, and deleted by the parent through the Main App.

We design our children's data practices to align with applicable children's privacy laws, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) in South Africa, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the GDPR's provisions on children's data, in each case where applicable. If you believe a device has been paired without appropriate parental consent, contact us and we will revoke it and delete the associated device record.

5. Data Stored on the Device

The only data the Kids App persists on the device is the device token (kept in the operating system's secure storage) and a temporary cache of the chore list for offline viewing. Uninstalling the app removes both. Revoking the device from the Main App invalidates the token server-side even if the app remains installed.

6. Third-Party Services

The Kids App relies on a small set of infrastructure providers to function:

  • Supabase — cloud database and API hosting for the household's chore data (the same backend used by the Main App).
  • Expo / EAS — the framework and build service used to develop and ship the app.

These providers process data only on our behalf to deliver the service. The Kids App contains no advertising networks, no social media SDKs, and no third-party analytics.

7. Data Security

All communication between the Kids App and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). The Kids App has no direct database access: every request goes through narrowly-scoped server functions that validate the device token and return only the data belonging to that one child. Device tokens are stored in the platform's secure keystore, not in plain storage.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Chore data belongs to the parent's household account and is retained as part of that account. Parents can delete individual chores, unpair devices, or delete the entire household account (including all children's data) through the Main App at any time. Revoked device tokens are invalidated immediately. Expired pairing codes are unusable after one hour.

9. Your Rights

Rights over a child's data — access, correction, deletion, and objection — are exercised by the child's parent or legal guardian, either directly through the Main App or by contacting us at the address below. We will respond to verified requests within a reasonable period as required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date above, and where the change meaningfully affects children's data we will notify parents through the Main App.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your child's data can be sent to:

Email: chore-equity@decoded.ltd
