Family Equity:

Rebuilt from the ground up

Your household, organized and shareable — privately.

Family Equity is now a native SwiftUI household organizer for children and pets, with family and moderated public posts, multiple-household sharing, reminders, and a companion experience for Apple Watch.

One private hub

Kids, pets and reminders in one place

Add a profile for each child or pet and track what matters day to day: school drop-off and pickup, sleep and wake times, activities, health notes, medicine, feeding times, important dates, and documents.

Reminders keep the family ahead of schedules, activities, medicine and important dates.

Profiles that matter

Everything about your kids and pets

Child profiles keep schedules, activities, school pickups, health notes, medicine, vitamins, important dates and documents together. Pet profiles cover feeding, health, medicine and records with the same clarity.

Share safely

Family moments, private or public

Keep updates inside your household with a private family feed, or opt in to a moderated public community feed. Public posts are checked automatically for safety before anyone outside your household can see them.

Built for real families

Co-parenting and multiple households

Invite a partner, grandparent or co-parent with a secure link or QR code. Join another household and switch between households whenever you need to — without mixing up anyone’s information.

Your data lives in your iCloud account and is only shared with people you invite.

On your wrist

Today’s events at a glance.

See upcoming school pickups, activities and important reminders right from Apple Watch.

Free & Premium

Start with the whole household.

Free covers 2 children, 2 pets, inviting 1 family member, and joining up to 2 households. Premium unlocks unlimited children and pets, unlimited family invites, more households, and priority notifications.
