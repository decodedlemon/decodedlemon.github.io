A seed, three cards a day, and two weeks to a tree.

Sprout is a cozy plant-care game with no timers to beat and nothing to grind. Every real day your garden gets one pack of three care cards. Play them on the plant that needs them most — then close the app and come back tomorrow.

The daily loop

A few minutes a day is the whole game.

Your plants drink, soak up sun and use up nutrients whether the app is open or not. Miss a few days and you’ll come back to thirsty plants and a bit of a mess — exactly like the real thing.

01
🌿

Draw

Three cards from a deck of nineteen, once every real day. The draw is weighted toward whatever meter is running critical — but it never just hands you a full top-off.

02
💎

Decide

Play a card now or bank it for a worse day — your shelf holds twelve. Aphids, ants and weeds move in from day three and drain the plant while they stay.

03
🌲

Grow

Six stages from Seed to Fully Grown, with the camera pulling back to keep the canopy in frame. Keep all three meters healthy and you get there in two weeks.

Built in Godot · iPhone + iPad

Every plant grows in its own way.

Each plant is procedurally generated from its own seed, so no two trees ever branch the same. Portrait, one hand, no loading screens between you and the garden.

The deck

Nineteen cards. Three a day.

Water, sun and nutrients are the three meters. Everything else in the deck is there to buy you time, clear a threat or tip a bad week back in your favour.

2 days Let any meter sit at zero for two days running and that plant is gone for good. Death is per-plant, not per-save — your other pots keep growing while you replant.

Quiet by design

Nothing here is trying to keep you here.

No ads. Ever.No banners, no interstitials, no rewarded video anywhere in the game.
No accountNo login, no internet needed. Your garden lives on your device and never leaves it.
One reminderA single optional notification at 9am, and nothing else. Decline it and the game still plays in full.
Nothing collectedNo analytics, no crash reporter, no ad network. Data Not Collected across every category.
No energy metersNo lives, no stamina, no paywalled progress. The free start is a complete game.
One-time purchasesExtra seeds, pots, card art and backdrops are permanent and restorable — never subscriptions.
Keep notesA built-in journal timestamps every entry with the in-game day, so you can read the whole run back later.
Single playerNo leaderboards, no social feed, no one else’s garden to measure yours against.
