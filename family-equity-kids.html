Family Equity Kids

Your chores, your stars! The child-friendly companion to Family Equity — kids see their own chores, tap them done, and celebrate with confetti.

Coming soon to theApp Store Coming soon onGoogle Play

Chores feel like a game

Kids get their very own app: just their chores, big friendly buttons, and points to earn. They see exactly what a grown-up has assigned — and nothing else. Every chore is worth stars that count on the family leaderboard in the parent app, so their help really counts.

Celebrate every win

Tap a chore when it's done and watch the confetti fly. Progress kids can see and feel proud of — points earned this week, chores done, and a friendly animal buddy (fox, frog, dino and more) to greet them every day. Oops-proof too: a tap can be undone, and only a grown-up can change or remove chores.

Kids can ask to help

Want to help more? Kids can send a chore idea to a grown-up right from the app — and see when it's approved. You stay in control: every request is approved or declined from the Family Equity parent app.

Safe by design

No ads, no purchases, no chat, no links — and no account or sign-up. A grown-up pairs the device in about ten seconds with a one-time magic code from the Family Equity app (requires Pro), and can unpair it at any time. The app collects no personal information from children.
