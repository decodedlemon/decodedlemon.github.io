Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Decoded Lemon ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Sprout (the "App"), a cozy, offline plant-care game. Sprout is built to collect nothing, and this policy explains exactly what that means in practice.

1. Information We Collect

None. Sprout has no account system, no login, and no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs of any kind. We do not collect your name, email address, device identifiers, location, or usage data — because the App never asks for it and has no server to send it to.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your garden — plant states, meters, growth progress, cosmetic unlocks, and journal entries — is saved locally to your device in a single save file, along with your preferences. This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us or anyone else, and is not backed up to any cloud service by the App itself. Deleting the App deletes this data. We have no way to access, view, or recover it.

3. In-App Purchases

Sprout offers optional in-app purchases (extra seeds, pots, card art, backdrops, and card packs), processed entirely through Apple's StoreKit or Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details — that information is handled directly by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We only receive confirmation that a purchase succeeded, which the App uses locally to unlock the relevant content.

4. Notifications

If you allow it, the App schedules one local daily reminder using your device's built-in notification system. This is generated and delivered entirely on-device — no notification service or third party is involved, and no data about you is sent anywhere to make it happen. You can decline or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings without affecting how the App works.

5. Children's Privacy

Sprout is rated for a general audience (4+ / Everyone) and contains no ads, no user-to-user content, and no data collection of any kind, making it suitable for children. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, no special handling is required for users of any age.

6. Third-Party Services

The only platform-level services Sprout touches are Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing (to process optional purchases) and your device's local notification scheduler. Neither transmits information about you to us. Sprout does not integrate advertising networks, analytics tools, or any other third-party SDK.

7. Your Rights

Because Sprout does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, there is no personal data of yours for us to access, correct, or delete — it never leaves your device in the first place. If you have questions about this, or believe that has changed, please contact us using the details in Section 8.

1. The App

Sprout is a single-player, fully offline plant-care game. No account, login, or internet connection is required to play. Your garden lives entirely on your device.

2. Time-Based Progression

Sprout advances one in-game day per real calendar day, based on your device's clock. Plants consume water, sunlight, and nutrients whether or not the App is open, and pests and weeds may appear over time. Progress after an extended absence is capped at 60 missed days. Changing your device's date or time may affect how the App calculates elapsed days.

3. Plant Loss

Neglecting a plant — letting any of its meters sit at zero for two consecutive in-game days — will cause that individual plant to die permanently. This is a core game mechanic, not a bug, and it is not reversible through customer support. Loss is per-plant only; it does not affect your other plants or your save file as a whole.
